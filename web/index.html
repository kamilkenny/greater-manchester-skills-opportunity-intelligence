DATA & METHODOLOGY

Transparent by design

Understand the sources, reporting periods, mapping rules and disclosure treatment behind GM SkillsFlow.

01

Apprenticeships

Department for Education apprenticeship statistics are used for borough-level supply, detailed subject areas and achievements.

02

Youth transition

DfE NEET and participation statistics are modelled at the combined 16-to-17 age-group headline level to avoid overlapping totals.

03

Labour market

ONS Nomis Annual Population Survey data provides employment, unemployment, inactivity and qualification indicators.

04

MBacc mapping

Subject Sector Areas are mapped to MBacc gateways through a governed analytical crosswalk. It is not represented as an official GMCA classification.

05

Reporting periods

Apprenticeship, youth-transition, labour-market and qualification statistics retain their actual publication periods.

06

Disclosure

Official suppressed or unavailable values remain null. GM SkillsFlow does not infer or reconstruct suppressed observations.
